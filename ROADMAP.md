# Roadmap

## Next: prove a useful community build

Choose one submitted problem with a resident or nonprofit willing to test it. Agree on a small result, a maintainer, a 4–5 hour build session and a follow-up check. Track use and feedback alongside votes. Dates are not yet confirmed.

## Make participation reliable

- Configure owner email notifications with a verified sender; exercise delivery and retries.
- Define contact retention, deletion requests and moderation turnaround.
- Keep demo participation open; add stronger abuse controls before promising one-person-one-vote.
- Label proposed ideas, prototypes and maintained tools clearly.

## Make reuse straightforward

- Move remaining city-specific copy, examples, social links, QR generation and research behind configuration.
- Add a documented direct-host authentication adapter before advertising one-click hosting.
- Add reproducible local API/browser checks and clean-install CI.
- Replace manual shared-code synchronization with a package only after a second community needs it.

## Resource research

Curated matches are available today. Fresh research, scheduled refreshes and grant applications are not. Add reviewed, capped research jobs with source dates, eligibility and budget controls before automating anonymous requests. Funding interest is not money collected or an awarded grant.
