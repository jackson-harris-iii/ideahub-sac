# Sacramento community project research

Checked October 20, 2026. Initial bounded primary-source review. Recommendations are our analysis; organizations listed are not confirmed partners. No grant award or application is implied.

## StoryPins

Start with a consent-based listening pilot in one neighborhood.

**Pilot:** Five resident-approved recordings, editable transcripts, approximate map pins and a clear removal request process.

**Funding outlook:** Connecting California is a plausible future match for public storytelling events with an eligible host. The published rounds are closed; no funding is committed.

**Validation:** Can five residents record, review and choose what to share? Can another resident find a story without exposing a private address?

**Constraints:** Obtain specific recording/publication permission, offer pseudonyms and coarse locations, separate public media permission from the code license, and make withdrawal practical.

**Resource needs:** A neighborhood host and oral-history adviser; Bilingual interviewers and transcript reviewers; Recording space, microphones and accessibility testing.

**Supporting sources:** [Sacramento Public Library · Sacramento Room](https://www.saclibrary.org/explore-services/local-history-sacramento-room), [California Humanities · Connecting California](https://calhum.org/connecting-california-grants/), [Sacramento Office of Arts & Culture](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/ccs/oac/funding-and-grants), [Small Organization Sustainability Fund](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/ccs/oac/funding-and-grants/small-organization-sustainability-fund), [Sacramento Region Community Foundation](https://www.sacregcf.org/nonprofits/).

## ReadySac

Build a five-minute, clearly labeled preparedness exercise using local official guidance.

**Pilot:** One heat/smoke scenario with branching choices, explanation of each choice and a checklist linking to SacAlert and official zone maps.

**Funding outlook:** SMUD Shine and future resilience coalitions are partner-led prospects. Current listed rounds are closed and a standalone app is not sufficient eligibility.

**Validation:** Can families explain their next preparedness step after the exercise? Check source dates and ensure no simulated message resembles a live warning.

**Constraints:** Never predict evacuation decisions or replace official alerts. Avoid competitive scoring that shames people with fewer resources; offer accessible and low-cost choices.

**Resource needs:** A preparedness educator to review every scenario; Family and disability-access reviewers; A library or community venue for a small facilitated test.

**Supporting sources:** [Sacramento Ready · preparedness resources](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/us/en/are-you-prepared.html), [SacAlert · official local alerts](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/us/en/are-you-prepared/emergency-alert-systems.html), [Sacramento County evacuation zone maps](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/us/en/prepare/evacuation-zone-maps.html), [Sacramento Ready · seasonal campaigns](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/), [Sac Metro Air District · air quality action charts](https://www.airquality.org/residents/public-outreach/wildfire-smoke-information/air-quality-action-charts), [Sac Metro Air District · wildfire smoke resources](https://www.airquality.org/Communications/Pages/Wildfire-Smoke-Information.aspx), [Sac Metro Air District · wildfire emergency plan](https://www.airquality.org/Communications/Documents/Wildfire%20Emergency%20Plan%20-%20FINAL.pdf), [National Weather Service API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api), [211 Sacramento · resource referrals](https://www.211sacramento.org/211/get-help/), [211 Sacramento · disaster relief](https://www.211sacramento.org/211/disaster-relief/), [SMUD Shine](https://www.smud.org/shine), [SMUD · 2026 Shine application announcement](https://www.smud.org/Corporate/About-us/News-and-Media/2026/2026/Apply-now-for-SMUDs-Shine-program), [Sacramento Region Community Foundation](https://www.sacregcf.org/nonprofits/), [California Grants Portal · Community Resilience Centers](https://www.grants.ca.gov/grant_categories/housing-community-economic-development/page/2/?order=desc&orderby=title&show_grants=active).

## Heat & Smoke Check-In

Pilot an opt-in buddy check-in with official condition links and visible timestamps.

**Pilot:** A condition summary, a voluntary “I’m okay / please check in” interaction, and links to verified local resources. Use simulated check-ins until a real coordinator is assigned.

**Funding outlook:** Seek in-kind coordination and venue support first. SMUD and resilience-center funding may support a broader eligible program in a future cycle.

**Validation:** Test stale-data handling and what happens when nobody responds. Agree on a real response protocol before recruiting vulnerable residents.

**Constraints:** A check-in is not emergency monitoring. Collect no diagnoses or precise home locations in the public board. Never promise response times without staffed coverage.

**Resource needs:** A community organization responsible for follow-up; Air-quality and preparedness reviewers; Multilingual outreach and accessible message design.

**Supporting sources:** [Sacramento Ready · preparedness resources](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/us/en/are-you-prepared.html), [SacAlert · official local alerts](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/us/en/are-you-prepared/emergency-alert-systems.html), [Sacramento Ready · seasonal campaigns](https://sacramentoready.saccounty.gov/), [Sac Metro Air District · air quality action charts](https://www.airquality.org/residents/public-outreach/wildfire-smoke-information/air-quality-action-charts), [Sac Metro Air District · wildfire smoke resources](https://www.airquality.org/Communications/Pages/Wildfire-Smoke-Information.aspx), [Sac Metro Air District · wildfire emergency plan](https://www.airquality.org/Communications/Documents/Wildfire%20Emergency%20Plan%20-%20FINAL.pdf), [EPA AirNow API](https://docs.airnowapi.org/), [National Weather Service API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api), [211 Sacramento · resource referrals](https://www.211sacramento.org/211/get-help/), [211 Sacramento · disaster relief](https://www.211sacramento.org/211/disaster-relief/), [SMUD Shine](https://www.smud.org/shine), [SMUD · 2026 Shine application announcement](https://www.smud.org/Corporate/About-us/News-and-Media/2026/2026/Apply-now-for-SMUDs-Shine-program), [Sacramento Region Community Foundation](https://www.sacregcf.org/nonprofits/), [California Grants Portal · Community Resilience Centers](https://www.grants.ca.gov/grant_categories/housing-community-economic-development/page/2/?order=desc&orderby=title&show_grants=active).

## SMS Resource Finder

Validate referral usefulness with 211 and a small permissioned directory before paying for live SMS.

**Pilot:** A web-based SMS-style conversation over 10 manually verified resources, with language choice, source dates and an easy human-referral exit.

**Funding outlook:** Nonprofit community-service support or a sponsored pilot is more credible than a grant for a generic chatbot. Verify each fund’s geography and applicant requirements.

**Validation:** Measure correct referrals and dead ends with sample requests. Test outdated hours, no matches and urgent needs; do not infer eligibility.

**Constraints:** Never scrape a directory on the assumption it is open data. Live SMS has consent, opt-out, carrier registration and per-message costs that require separate setup.

**Resource needs:** A referral partner and named directory steward; Multilingual residents testing comprehension; A permitted data feed and a budget for messaging.

**Supporting sources:** [211 Sacramento · resource referrals](https://www.211sacramento.org/211/get-help/), [211 Sacramento · disaster relief](https://www.211sacramento.org/211/disaster-relief/), [Sacramento Food Bank · Food Finder](https://www.sacramentofoodbank.org/find-food), [Open Referral · Human Services Data Specification](https://docs.openreferral.org/en/v3.3.1/hsds/overview.html), [Sacramento Region Community Foundation](https://www.sacregcf.org/nonprofits/).

## Recommended allocation of effort

Start the board pilot by finding an accountable local host for each concept. StoryPins has a small, consent-led pilot; ReadySac needs expert-reviewed learning content; Heat & Smoke needs a staffed response protocol; SMS Resource Finder needs data permission and a directory steward. None should be advertised as an operational emergency or eligibility service.

Do not budget around currently closed grants. First seek in-kind venues, subject-matter review and translation support; then match a qualified nonprofit/fiscal sponsor to a future grant cycle. Confirm match requirements with SMUD because published materials conflict. The state portal’s active label conflicts with its September 25 deadline; treat that round as closed.
